# Risk register

| ID | Risk | Severity | Owner | Mitigation or decision | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `R-001` | Describe the observable risk | Critical / High / Medium / Low | Role or person | Concrete next action | Open / Mitigated / Accepted |

## Rules

- A critical risk blocks a Go decision until it is resolved or formally accepted
  by the accountable release owner.
- A risk without an owner is still open.
- Record evidence links in the private project repository when they identify a
  customer, environment, user, or implementation detail.
