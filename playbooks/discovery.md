# Playbook: discovery and scope confirmation

## Objective

Create a factual baseline before any implementation change.

## Procedure

1. Define the business outcome, users, success evidence, and delivery owner.
2. Inventory apps, automations, data sources, integrations, and dependencies.
3. Record unknowns explicitly; do not fill gaps through assumption.
4. Classify risks by severity and assign an owner and next action.
5. Confirm the minimum scope that can be tested safely.

## Exit evidence

- Scope, assumptions, and open decisions are documented.
- Data and access dependencies have an accountable owner.
- Critical risks have either been resolved or blocked with a visible decision.
